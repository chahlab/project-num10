Book recommendations:

  • Pride and Prejudice by Jane Austen

    • A classic novel following the turbulent relationship between Elizabeth Bennet, the daughter of a country gentleman, and Fitzwilliam Darcy, a rich aristocratic landowner, as they deal with issues of manners, upbringing, morality, education, and marriage in 19th-century landed gentry England.

  • 1984 by George Orwell

    • A dystopian social science fiction novel and cautionary tale, warning of the dangers of totalitarianism and extreme political ideology. The story takes place in a society under constant surveillance by the Party, led by Big Brother, where independent thinking is a crime.

  • Moby-Dick by Herman Melville

    • A novel about the voyage of the whaling ship Pequod, led by Captain Ahab, who is obsessed with seeking revenge on Moby Dick, a giant white sperm whale that had previously destroyed Ahab's ship and severed his leg. The story explores themes of obsession, revenge, and humanity's struggle against nature.

  • To Kill a Mockingbird by Harper Lee

    • A novel set in the American South during the 1930s, focusing on themes of racial injustice and moral growth. The story is told through the eyes of Scout Finch, a young girl whose father, Atticus Finch, is a lawyer defending a black man falsely accused of raping a white woman.

  • The Great Gatsby by F. Scott Fitzgerald

    • A novel set in the Jazz Age on Long Island, near New York City, that critiques the American Dream. The story follows the mysterious millionaire Jay Gatsby and his obsession with the beautiful former debutante Daisy Buchanan, exploring themes of decadence, idealism, resistance to change, social upheaval, and excess.

About The Authors:

  • jane austen

    • Jane Austen was an English novelist known primarily for her six major novels, which interpret, critique and comment upon the British landed gentry at the end of the 18th century.

  • george orwell

    • George Orwell was an English novelist, essayist, journalist and critic. His work is marked by lucid prose, awareness of social injustice, opposition to totalitarianism, and support for democratic socialism.

  • herman melville

    • Herman Melville was an American novelist and short story writer. He is best known for his novel Moby-Dick.

  • harper lee

    • Harper Lee was an American novelist widely considered one of the greatest writers of the 20th century. Her novel To Kill a Mockingbird is a classic of modern American literature.

  • f. scott fitzgerald

    • F. Scott Fitzgerald was an American novelist and short story writer. He is best known for his novel The Great Gatsby.
